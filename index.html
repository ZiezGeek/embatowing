Based local, ready fast

Rooted in Embalenhle. Running across Mpumalanga.

Emba Towing & Recovery (Pty) Ltd is a family-run recovery operation based on Joe Mkhwanazi Crescent, Embalenhle. Our fleet of flatbeds and recovery bakkies works around the clock, so a breakdown at 2pm and a collision at 2am get the same response.

We know these roads — the N17, the routes into Evander and Secunda, and everything in between — which means less time waiting on the shoulder and more time getting home.
